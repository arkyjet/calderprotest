First crewed test mission

VGoFT

Vanguard Orbital Flight Test is Horizon Space's first full demonstration of its shuttle system in crewed orbital flight. The mission is built around proving that the vehicle can safely launch, operate in orbit, manage its payload bay systems, de-orbit, and return for runway landing.

The flight carries Jebadiah Kerman and Valentina Kerman on a short but high-value test profile designed to validate the shuttle for later station, logistics, and deployment missions.

Mission goal

Primary objective

Demonstrate that Vanguard can complete a full end-to-end crewed orbital mission and return safely as an operational shuttle.

For a first flight, the best goal is not a complicated station visit or satellite deployment. It should be a clean systems-validation mission that proves the shuttle works as a reusable spacecraft. That gives the program a believable foundation and makes later missions feel earned.

Success criteria

Reach stable orbit.
Open and close payload bay doors.
Check orbital manoeuvring and RCS control.
Support crew operations in orbit.
Perform de-orbit burn.
Complete controlled atmospheric re-entry.
Land intact on runway.

Why this mission makes sense

Realistic first step

A test flight should focus on proving the spacecraft itself. That means keeping the profile disciplined and making every major phase of shuttle flight part of the mission.

Perfect crew pairing

Jebadiah can serve as mission commander and test pilot, while Valentina acts as pilot and systems specialist. That gives the mission a proper two-person flight test structure.

Sets up future flights

Once VGoFT succeeds, Horizon Space can move into more ambitious missions like satellite release, rendezvous practice, station resupply, lab flights, or crew rotation.

Suggested mission profile
Launch
Ascent to orbit. Perform the first crewed launch of Vanguard and insert into a stable low orbit around Kerbin.
Orbit day 1
Systems checkout. Test payload bay door operations, thermal configuration, RCS handling, crew procedures, and shuttle attitude control in multiple orientations.
Orbit day 1–2
Flight handling demonstration. Conduct a small orbital manoeuvre, verify tracking and guidance performance, and photograph the vehicle and payload bay configuration.
De-orbit
Return sequence. Close the bay, perform de-orbit burn, manage entry attitude, and fly the shuttle through a controlled hypersonic and subsonic descent.
Landing
Runway touchdown. Finish the mission with a clean landing to prove Vanguard is ready to move from experimental status toward routine service.
Possible follow-on missions

VG-2

First payload deployment mission, carrying a small satellite or probe from the payload bay.

VG-3

Rendezvous and proximity operations test with a target vehicle or station module.

VG-4

Operational logistics flight, delivering equipment or crew to a permanent Horizon Space outpost.
